# Scoring Rubric

This is an example rubric. Copy it to `~/.agent-skills/rubric.md` and edit it to match your priorities.

Each axis should have a name, what "good" looks like, and what "bad" looks like. The analyzer will rate each axis and cite the JD text that supports the rating.

---

## Axes

### Reporting line

- **Strong:** Reports to engineering or product leadership.
- **Weak:** Reports to marketing, comms, or a non-technical function.
- **Note:** If the reporting line can't be determined from the JD, rate as "unclear" and flag it as something to ask about.

### Product loop

- **Strong:** Real product influence — shapes what gets built, contributes to roadmaps, writes specs, or owns developer experience end-to-end.
- **Weak:** Walled-off docs or content function with no product input. Work is handed down, not collaborated on.
- **Note:** This applies even when the role sits under engineering. A docs team inside eng can still be a silo.

### AI posture

- **Strong:** Treats AI tooling (copilots, code generation, AI-assisted workflows) as a normal part of how work gets done. Mentions AI in the stack, the workflow, or the culture without hype.
- **Weak:** No mention of AI, or actively signals resistance/distrust of AI tooling. Bonus points deducted for "AI will not replace us" energy in the JD.

### Build-first

- **Strong:** Wants someone who writes code — sample apps, reference implementations, SDKs, CLI tools, internal tooling. Hands-on building is core to the role.
- **Weak:** Primarily writing docs, guides, or tutorials about things other people built. The role talks about technology but doesn't create it.
